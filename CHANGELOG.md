# Changelog

Changes to Couchwave. The newest are at the top; "Unreleased" is what's been added since the last release.

## Unreleased

### Added

- **Audio only**: a headphones button next to full screen turns the video off just for you, so only the
  sound plays and the video isn't downloaded (saves data). Click it again to bring the picture back, in
  sync with the sound. It's remembered in your browser.
- **Loading spinner** on the video while the picture loads (after turning audio only off, when a song
  starts, or while it buffers), instead of a plain black screen.
